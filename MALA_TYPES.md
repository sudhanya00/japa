# Mala Bead Types - Complete Guide

## Overview
Japa now supports **10 traditional mala bead types** from various spiritual traditions. Names use Hinglish (Hindi-English) for cultural authenticity while remaining accessible.

---

## 🕉️ Hindu/Vedic Tradition

### 1. **Rudraksh** (रुद्राक्ष)
- **Type**: Sacred seed
- **Color**: Dark brown (#8B4513 → #654321)
- **Tradition**: Shaivism (Lord Shiva)
- **Benefits**: Spiritual protection, meditation, stress relief
- **Popular for**: Om Namah Shivaya, Mahamrityunjaya mantra
- **Cultural significance**: Most sacred bead in Hindu tradition

### 2. **Tulsi** (तुलसी)
- **Type**: Holy basil wood
- **Color**: Light brown (#8B7355 → #6B5845)
- **Tradition**: Vaishnavism (Lord Vishnu/Krishna)
- **Benefits**: Devotion, peace, health
- **Popular for**: Hare Krishna mantra, Vishnu mantras
- **Cultural significance**: Sacred to Lord Krishna devotees

### 3. **Kamal Gatta** (कमल गट्टा)
- **Type**: Lotus seed
- **Color**: Tan/beige (#D2B48C → #C4A57B)
- **Tradition**: General Hindu/Buddhist
- **Benefits**: Purity, enlightenment, spiritual growth
- **Popular for**: Lakshmi mantras, general meditation
- **Cultural significance**: Lotus symbolizes purity rising from mud

### 4. **Chandan** (चन्दन)
- **Type**: Sandalwood
- **Color**: Pale golden yellow (#E8D4A0 → #D4BC84)
- **Tradition**: All Hindu traditions
- **Benefits**: Cooling effect, peace, mental clarity
- **Popular for**: General mantras, peaceful meditation
- **Cultural significance**: Sacred wood used in pujas

## 💎 Crystal/Gemstone Tradition

### 5. **Sphatik** (स्फटिक)
- **Type**: Crystal quartz
- **Color**: Clear white (#F0F0F0 → #E0E0E0)
- **Tradition**: Hindu/Universal
- **Benefits**: Clarity, amplification of intentions, Saraswati worship
- **Popular for**: Gayatri mantra, knowledge-seeking mantras
- **Cultural significance**: Pure crystal represents clarity of mind

### 6. **Amethyst**
- **Type**: Purple crystal
- **Color**: Violet purple (#9966CC → #7744AA)
- **Tradition**: Universal/New Age
- **Benefits**: Crown chakra, meditation, spiritual awareness
- **Popular for**: Chakra meditation, spiritual awakening
- **Cultural significance**: Associated with higher consciousness

## 🌊 Jyotish (Vedic Astrology) Tradition

### 7. **Moonga** (मूंगा)
- **Type**: Red coral
- **Color**: Coral red (#FF6B6B → #E85555)
- **Tradition**: Vedic astrology (Mars)
- **Benefits**: Courage, vitality, Mars energy, physical strength
- **Popular for**: Mars-related mantras, overcoming obstacles
- **Cultural significance**: Recommended for Mars (Mangal) remedies

### 8. **Hakik - Red** (लाल हकीक)
- **Type**: Red agate
- **Color**: Deep red (#C44536 → #A53828)
- **Tradition**: Islamic/Sufi + Hindu
- **Benefits**: Grounding, protection, root chakra
- **Popular for**: General protection, grounding mantras
- **Cultural significance**: Cross-cultural spiritual stone

### 9. **Hakik - Black** (काला हकीक)
- **Type**: Black agate
- **Color**: Deep black (#2C2C2C → #1A1A1A)
- **Tradition**: Islamic/Sufi + Hindu
- **Benefits**: Protection from negativity, strength, Saturn energy
- **Popular for**: Protection mantras, overcoming fears
- **Cultural significance**: Powerful protective stone

### 10. **Hakik - Green** (हरा हकीक)
- **Type**: Green agate
- **Color**: Forest green (#4A7C59 → #3A6347)
- **Tradition**: Islamic/Sufi + Hindu
- **Benefits**: Healing, prosperity, Mercury energy, heart chakra
- **Popular for**: Healing mantras, prosperity prayers
- **Cultural significance**: Associated with growth and abundance

## 🎯 Philosophy

### Why Hinglish Names?

**Cultural Authenticity**
- Respects Indian spiritual tradition
- Honors original Sanskrit/Hindi names
- More meaningful to practitioners

**Accessibility**
- Still readable for English speakers
- Phonetic spelling (easy to pronounce)
- No diacritics (simple to type)

**Examples**
- ❌ "Crystal Quartz" → ✅ "Sphatik"
- ❌ "Coral" → ✅ "Moonga"
- ❌ "Agate" → ✅ "Hakik"

### Spiritual, Not Religious

**Inclusive Design**
- Hindu (Rudraksh, Tulsi, etc.)
- Buddhist (Kamal Gatta)
- Islamic/Sufi (Hakik)
- Universal (Amethyst)

**All traditions welcome** 🙏
